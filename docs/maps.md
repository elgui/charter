How they connect (`docs/assets/maps/functional.svg`): units cite what is agreed (`cap.agree` → `cap.unit`); each change a unit makes crosses the gates (`cap.unit` → `cap.gate`); the gates log every finding and the units carry the evidence that reports are computed from (→ `cap.report`); a chain runs units one after another and waits on the owner (`cap.chain`); the owner signs and rules what is agreed and holds the keys that define the gates (`cap.owner`); the fleet tooling installs all of it (`cap.fleet`) (`docs/method.md` § The method on one page).

### `cap.agree` 1. Write down what is agreed

What is sold, the road and every design choice live in the repository, one file each, where a machine can check them. On the client track `charter/scope.md` summarises the signed devis, and work outside it is a change request that the owner approves. `charter/rails.md` is the road in order, and adding an item to it is the recorded re-plan. A decision is one file with its reason and its expiry; code that depends on it carries `charter:<id>`, and its `check:` command runs on the files its `binds:` names. A lesson that binds the project is a decision with `kind: lesson`. (`docs/method.md` § Artifacts and their owners; `check_scope` in `package/.charter/checks.py`; `package/.charter/rails.py`)

- **Who acts:** `owner`, `agent` (section 1, `docs/maps.md`).
- **Entry points:**
  - `charter-decision skill`: "record the decision": writes `charter/decisions/<id>.md` (`package/.claude/skills/charter-decision/SKILL.md`)
  - `charter decisions index`: regenerates the decision index (`cmd_decisions` in `package/.charter/bin/charter`)
  - `templates`: scope, rails, decision, change (`package/charter/templates/`)
  - `charter rule | approve | rail`: the owner's ruling, in one commit (`package/.charter/owner.py`)
- **Files it reads or writes in a project:** `charter/scope.md`, `charter/rails.md`, `charter/decisions/<id>.md`, `charter/decisions/INDEX.md`, `charter/changes/<id>.md` (`docs/package.md` § What lands in a project).
- **Rests on the concepts:** `c.track` Track, `c.scope` Scope, `c.change` Change request, `c.rails` Rails, `c.decision` Decision, `c.owner` Owner, `c.agent` Agent session (section 3, `docs/maps.md`).
- **Read more:** `docs/method.md`, `docs/gates.md`.

### `cap.unit` 2. Work in units that prove themselves

Each deliverable is one file that says what it may touch, how it will be checked, and what happened. A unit (a tasket in `.taskets/`) cites a scope item or a rail, declares its `perimeter:`, and lists acceptance criteria, each a probe that fails before the work and passes after. `## Evidence` names a commit or a `$ command` run, and the dated `## Log` is what the next session resumes from. `charter next` says what comes next; `charter unit start` attributes the session's edits and cost to the unit. (`docs/daily-use.md` § The loop; `package/charter/templates/unit.md`)

- **Who acts:** `agent` (section 1, `docs/maps.md`).
- **Entry points:**
  - `charter-unit skill`: "start unit <id>", "finish the unit" (`package/.claude/skills/charter-unit/SKILL.md`)
  - `charter next`: the road, the next units, what waits on the owner; writes nothing (`cmd_next` in `package/.charter/bin/charter`)
  - `charter unit start | stop | show`: the session's active unit, and a unit's perimeter fit (`cmd_unit` in `package/.charter/bin/charter`)
  - `charter probe <id>`: runs the probes, prints the `$ command` evidence lines (`cmd_probe` in `package/.charter/bin/charter`)
- **Files it reads or writes in a project:** `.taskets/<id>.md`, `charter/templates/unit.md` (`docs/package.md` § What lands in a project).
- **Rests on the concepts:** `c.unit` Unit, `c.perimeter` Perimeter, `c.acceptance` Acceptance, `c.probe` Probe, `c.evidence` Evidence, `c.log` Log, `c.accepted` Accepted, `c.agent` Agent session (section 3, `docs/maps.md`).
- **Read more:** `docs/daily-use.md`, `package/charter/templates/unit.md`.

### `cap.gate` 3. Check every change, three times

One engine judges each change as the agent edits, at commit, and in CI from a clean clone. Claude Code hooks refuse an edit before it lands; the git pre-commit hook judges what is staged, whoever commits; CI runs the same checks at merge. A hard gate refuses and names its override, the honest way past it. Everything else warns and goes to the gate log, the evidence for turning a warning into a gate. A gate fails open on its own errors, except a config it cannot read, which is refused. (`docs/gates.md`; `package/.charter/checks.py`)

- **Who acts:** `machines`, `agent` (section 1, `docs/maps.md`).
- **Entry points:**
  - `Claude Code hooks`: SessionStart, PreToolUse, PostToolUse, Stop, SessionEnd (`package/.charter/settings-fragment.json`)
  - `git pre-commit`: `charter check --staged`, whoever commits (`package/.charter/githooks/pre-commit`)
  - `CI`: `charter check --all --ci`, `charter anchors` (`package/.github/workflows/charter.yml`)
  - `charter setup | config | log`: activate the hook per clone; what the gates read; what they did (`package/.charter/bin/charter`)
- **Files it reads or writes in a project:** `.charter/checks.py`, `.charter/hooks/`, `.charter/githooks/pre-commit`, `.github/workflows/charter.yml`, `charter/config.json`, `.git/charter/log.jsonl` (`docs/package.md` § What lands in a project).
- **Rests on the concepts:** `c.gate` Gate, `c.line` Three lines, `c.override` Override, `c.keys` Owner-only keys, `c.gatelog` Gate log, `c.track` Track, `c.accepted` Accepted (section 3, `docs/maps.md`).
- **Read more:** `docs/gates.md`.

### `cap.owner` 4. Keep the owner in charge

The files and fields that define the gates are the owner's. Agents propose; the owner rules in one command. An agent's edit of an owner-only file or field is refused in the session, at a commit made inside Claude Code, and by the author check in CI. `charter owner` lists what waits on the owner, each with its command; `charter rule`, `approve` and `rail` write a ruling and commit it. An agent stages an owner-only change and runs `charter handover`, which prints the owner's one line. At `routine`, `answers` or `open`, the owner's delegate takes the acts the level allows. (`package/.claude/rules/charter.md:28-51`; `docs/gates.md` § Owner-only changes)

- **Who acts:** `owner`, `delegate`, `agent` (section 1, `docs/maps.md`).
- **Entry points:**
  - `charter owner`: the queue that waits on the owner, with the command for each (`queue` in `package/.charter/owner.py`)
  - `charter rule | approve | rail`: rule a decision, approve a change, set a rail, in one commit (`package/.charter/owner.py`)
  - `charter handover`: records an owner-only change and prints the owner's line (`record_handover` in `package/.charter/lib.py`)
  - `CHARTER_OWNER=1 … owner commit`: the owner's line: `CHARTER_OWNER=1 python3 <root>/.charter/bin/charter owner commit` commits exactly what was handed over (`docs/design/owner-handover.md`)
  - `charter delegate plan | level`: what the delegate may take here, and the level (`package/.charter/delegate.py`)
- **Files it reads or writes in a project:** `charter/config.json (owner, delegate.level)`, `.git/charter/handover.json` (`docs/package.md` § What lands in a project).
- **Rests on the concepts:** `c.owner` Owner, `c.keys` Owner-only keys, `c.delegate` Owner's delegate, `c.decision` Decision, `c.change` Change request, `c.rails` Rails (section 3, `docs/maps.md`).
- **Read more:** `docs/design/owner-delegate.md`, `docs/design/owner-handover.md`.

### `cap.report` 5. Say what is true, and what it cost

Progress, acceptance and cost are computed from the repository, and every claim points at something a reader can open. `charter synth` writes `charter/progress.md`: scope and rails with their units, done and accepted apart from done and not accepted, work in progress, open decisions, perimeter fit and cost; it refuses to write if its own anchor check fails. `charter anchors` checks any deliverable: each claim line carries an anchor or admits it is reported or an inference. The SessionEnd hook records tokens per session, and `charter ledger fold` prices them into cost per accepted unit. (`docs/method.md` § The progress synthesis, § Accepted results, § Cost per accepted result at this scale)

- **Who acts:** `agent`, `owner`, `client` (section 1, `docs/maps.md`).
- **Entry points:**
  - `charter-progress skill`: "where does this stand?" (`package/.claude/skills/charter-progress/SKILL.md`)
  - `charter synth [--write]`: the progress synthesis, anchored (`cmd_synth` in `package/.charter/bin/charter`)
  - `charter anchors <file>`: every claim line anchored, or admitted (`check_anchors` in `package/.charter/checks.py`)
  - `charter ledger fold`: this machine's sessions, priced, folded per unit (`cmd_ledger` in `package/.charter/bin/charter`)
  - `--json on next, unit show, owner`: the same answers for a tool (`docs/package.md` § Versioning and upgrade (0.4.13))
- **Files it reads or writes in a project:** `charter/progress.md (generated)`, `charter/ledger.jsonl`, `.git/charter/sessions.jsonl` (`docs/package.md` § What lands in a project).
- **Rests on the concepts:** `c.synth` Progress synthesis, `c.anchor` Anchor, `c.ledger` Ledger, `c.accepted` Accepted, `c.gatelog` Gate log, `c.evidence` Evidence (section 3, `docs/maps.md`).
- **Read more:** `docs/method.md`.

### `cap.chain` 6. Run the road unattended

A chain of fresh sessions works the road unit after unit, each checking the last, reviewed group by group. `/charter-chain` starts a chain, on a new branch `chain/<id>` when run from main. Each session receives the chain rules verbatim, verifies its predecessor, works, and hands over before 25% of its context window; past 30% the hook refuses every tool but the handoff, the unit files, git and the charter CLI. `charter chain next` validates the handoff, re-runs the probes and opens the next session. At the end of each group (a rail item) Codex reviews its commits and the next session answers every finding. When the road needs the owner, the chain waits with every act in `owner.md`; the owner runs them with `charter owner run` and types `/resume-chain ok`. (`docs/daily-use.md` § Running units in a chain; `docs/design/continuous-chain.md`)

- **Who acts:** `agent`, `reviewer`, `owner` (section 1, `docs/maps.md`).
- **Entry points:**
  - `/charter-chain`: start a chain from a unit and an instruction (`package/.claude/skills/charter-chain/SKILL.md`)
  - `charter chain start | next | status | stop | report`: run, hand over, watch, end, report (`package/.charter/chain.py`)
  - `charter owner run`: the owner runs a waiting chain's acts (`package/.charter/owner_acts.py`)
  - `/resume-chain ok | skip`: the owner's resume, performed by the hook (`package/.claude/commands/resume-chain.md`)
- **Files it reads or writes in a project:** `.charter/chain-rules.md`, `charter/templates/handoff.md`, `charter/templates/owner.md`, `.git/charter/chain/<id>/` (`docs/package.md` § What lands in a project).
- **Rests on the concepts:** `c.chain` Chain, `c.reviewer` Independent reviewer, `c.rails` Rails, `c.log` Log, `c.unit` Unit, `c.owner` Owner (section 3, `docs/maps.md`).
- **Read more:** `docs/design/continuous-chain.md`, `docs/daily-use.md`.

### `cap.fleet` 7. Install, upgrade and oversee projects

One checkout of this repository installs the package into any project, keeps it current and watches the fleet. `charter-pkg install` copies the package into a project, records the hash of each owned file in `.charter/manifest.json` and stages the result for the owner's commit; `upgrade` shows each diff; `survey` measures a project before a migration; `fleet` lists every charter project and upgrades the ones behind. Each fresh clone runs `charter setup` once to activate the git hook. The machine layer, installed once per user, adds a SessionStart hook that says when a project is behind, and the CharterAdmin skill with its Migrate workflow. (`docs/install.md`; `docs/package.md`; `machine/README.md`)

- **Who acts:** `owner`, `agent` (section 1, `docs/maps.md`).
- **Entry points:**
  - `charter-pkg install | upgrade | uninstall | status`: the package into one project (`bin/charter-pkg`)
  - `charter-pkg fleet`: every project: list, --upgrade, --commit, --levels (`do_fleet` in `bin/charter-pkg`)
  - `charter-pkg survey | instructions`: measure a project before a migration; read-only (`bin/charter_survey.py`)
  - `charter-pkg machine install`: the hook and CharterAdmin skill in ~/.claude (`bin/charter_machine.py`)
  - `charter setup`: once per clone: activate the git hook (`cmd_setup` in `package/.charter/bin/charter`)
- **Files it reads or writes in a project:** `package/ → <project>/.charter, charter/, .claude/, .github/`, `.charter/manifest.json`, `charter/VERSION`, `~/.claude/charter-local.md` (`docs/package.md` § What lands in a project).
- **Rests on the concepts:** `c.track` Track, `c.keys` Owner-only keys, `c.owner` Owner (section 3, `docs/maps.md`).
- **Read more:** `docs/install.md`, `docs/package.md`, `machine/README.md`.

## 7. A chain of sessions

The picture is `docs/assets/maps/chain.svg` (`docs/daily-use.md` § Running units in a chain; `docs/design/continuous-chain.md`).

1. `/charter-chain <unit> <instruction>` starts a chain; `charter chain start` creates `chain/<id>` when run on main and opens session 1 (`resolve_launcher` in `package/.charter/chain.py`).
2. Each session receives `.charter/chain-rules.md` verbatim, verifies its predecessor's work, works unit after unit, and hands over at 25% of its context window; past 30% the hook refuses every tool but writing the handoff or a unit file, `git` and the charter CLI (`docs/gates.md` § Chain budget and hand-over (0.4.9)).
3. `charter chain next` refuses a malformed handoff, records what the session committed, re-runs the probes of the units it closed, runs the gates on its change set, and opens the next session (`cmd_next` in `package/.charter/chain.py`).
4. A group is the units of one rail item. When it ends, Codex reviews its commits in a worktree; the next session answers each finding as fixed or dismissed before the next group starts, and the group is cleared at a commit the owner can merge up to (`package/.charter/chain_groups.py`).
5. When the road needs the owner, the session writes every act into `owner.md` and waits; Codex reads the acts and sends back what an agent could do; the owner runs them with `charter owner run` and types `/resume-chain ok`, which closes the lines whose acts passed (`package/.charter/owner_acts.py`).

| State | Meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `running` | a session works; the next opens at its hand-over | `package/.charter/chain.py` |
| `waiting` | a session needs the owner; every act is in owner.md | `docs/gates.md` § Chain budget and hand-over (0.4.9) |
| `halted` | a session asked a question, or a review could not run | `docs/daily-use.md` § Running units in a chain |

| `complete` | the road is finished, on a clean tree | `docs/daily-use.md` § Running units in a chain |

## 8. Where the gates stop

- A gate checks that evidence is cited and resolves, not that it proves the claim; the reviewer and the owner judge that (`docs/gates.md` § What a gate cannot see).
- A Bash command is not an Edit or a Write: the session hooks do not see a file it changes, the git hook and CI do (`docs/gates.md` § What a gate cannot see).
- A gate that crashes lets the change through and logs `decision=error`; `--no-verify` skips the git hook, and CI runs the checks again (`docs/method.md` § The hard gates and why a warning would not do).
- A merge, a deployment, a push and a purchase are actions, not files: no gate sees them, the rules file carries them (`docs/gates.md` § What a gate cannot see).
- The lock that holds against an agent acting as the owner is outside the repository: branch protection and code owners (`docs/gates.md` § Owner-only changes).
