# Trial record, 2026-10-04: a chain's groups and their review (0.4.8)

The test bed came from `bin/make-testbed` on the prototype track, with its unit replaced by a road of two rails: `r1` holds t1 (`greet` in `app/greet.py`) and t2 (`slugify` in `app/slug.py`), and `r2` holds t3 (`median` in `app/stats.py`). Each unit had one probe that tests one case: `slugify('Hello, World!')` and `median([3, 1, 2])`, nothing about Unicode or even lengths. The package was this branch's working tree. The test bed lived in the session scratchpad and is not committed. Each chain's event log, handoffs, verification reports, reviews and `chain report` are copied under `docs/trials/2026-10-04-groups/<chain id>/`. The mission was the owner's words of the 0.4.7 trial: "go with unit t1. Optimize context window, and as long as things go well, iterate on the next unit until it's done."

The sessions ran headless (`--launcher headless`, `claude -p`) on the owner's lean profile at `--model sonnet --effort low`. The reviews ran at the default, `codex exec review` at effort `high`, codex-cli 0.147.0, from the runner that `chain next` started inside each session's Bash command (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-groups/20261004-110840/events.log:1`, `$ codex --version`).

What the run showed:

- **The runner works from inside a session.** Each review was started by a headless session's `chain next`, ran Codex in a detached process with network access, and launched the next session or completed the chain on its own (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-groups/20261004-110840/events.log:5-7`, `:14-17`).
- **The worktree.** Codex's paths came back relative to the repository (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-groups/20261004-110840/g01-review.md:5`), and `git worktree list` in the test bed listed only the main checkout once the chain was over (`$ git worktree list`, 2026-10-04).
- **The answer gate and the answering session.** s02 gave the finding its whole session and changed only `app/slug.py` and `.taskets/t2.md` (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-groups/20261004-110840/s02-facts.md:2-5`).
- **Cost.** About 0.55 USD for the three sessions, estimated from `sessions.jsonl` with the package's price rows (`$ python3 .charter/bin/charter synth` in the test bed). The two reviews ran on the owner's Codex subscription, which records no price.

## A second run, after the cross-review

A Codex cross-review of the change (`codex exec review --uncommitted`, 321 s), checked finding by finding, led to changes in the runner and the gates: the reviewer runs in a process group of its own, which a timeout kills whole; the runner keeps its pid until the chain's state is saved; a review that ends after a stop records and ends nothing; each review of a group is kept; one answer line per finding, starting with its id; nothing uncommitted crosses a group's end. Each change has its test in `tests/test_groups_0_4_8.py`, class `AfterTheCrossReview`.

The same trial ran again on that engine, chain 20261004-112911, from a fresh test bed. It went the same way: s01 closed t1 and t2 at 20%; the review of `r1` took 73 s and found the same Unicode defect in `slugify` (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-groups/20261004-112911/g01-review.md:5`); s02 fixed it in two commits, the fix and the unit's evidence, and answered `R1 fixed` (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-groups/20261004-112911/s02-facts.md:1-3`, `docs/trials/2026-10-04-groups/20261004-112911/s02-handoff.md`); s03 closed t3; the review of `r2` took 75 s with no findings, and the chain ended `complete` (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-groups/20261004-112911/events.log:5-17`).

## What it did not show

- Tabs. The sessions ran headless. Through the spawn plugin, the runner launches the next session with the same `launch` as `chain next` and the environment of the session that started it, so it opens a tab where `chain next` would (`start_review`, `launch_next` in `package/.charter/chain_groups.py`) (inference, not run: a scratchpad test bed stops each new tab at the folder-trust menu, `docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain.md` § Visible tabs).
- A review that cannot run, a stop during a review, and a halt on a finding. The tests cover them with a fake reviewer (`tests/test_groups_0_4_8.py`, `EndAndFailure`).
- Timing on a larger group: both groups were one or two small functions (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-groups/20261004-110840/s01-facts.md`, `docs/trials/2026-10-04-groups/20261004-110840/s03-facts.md`).
