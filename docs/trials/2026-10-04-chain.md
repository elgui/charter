# Trial record, 2026-10-04: the continuous chain

The test bed came from `bin/make-testbed` on the prototype track. Its unit was replaced by three units, t1 to t3. Each adds one small function (`app/greet.py`, `app/slug.py`, `app/stats.py`) and has one probe that failed at the baseline (`$ python3 .charter/bin/charter probe t1` → exit 1, likewise t2 and t3). The package was this branch's working tree. The test bed lived in the session scratchpad and is not committed. Each chain's event log, handoffs and verification reports are copied under `docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain/`. The mission, every time, was the owner's words: "go with unit t1. Optimize context window, and as long as things go well, iterate on the next unit until it's done."

Sessions ran on the owner's lean profile, `~/.claude-lean/README.md`, with its user hooks, at `--model sonnet --effort low`.

## Before the chain: what a session starts with

A probe session in the test bed asked Bash for `$CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID` (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain/probe-session.summary.json`):

- the first response's context was 32,743 tokens before any work: system prompt, tools, skills, `CLAUDE.md` and charter's SessionStart context (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain/probe-session.summary.json`, `responses[0]`);
- the Bash command saw `CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID` equal to the session's own id, as the init line gives it (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain/probe-session.summary.json`, `result` against `init_session_id`). `charter chain next` and `chain join` rely on that.

What the runs showed:

- **Join.** Every session joined within 1 to 3 seconds of its launch, so `UserPromptSubmit` fires on a `claude -p` prompt and carries the marker line to the hook (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain/20261004-005317/events.log:3`, the same at lines 6 and 9).
- **Budget.** The 5-point notes reached the model. s02 wrote "Context was already at 24% after verification" (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain/20261004-005317/s02-handoff.md:14`). The first chain's s01 ended on "the hook reported 16% at the last reading" (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain/20261004-005204/s01-final-message.txt:15`).
- **Verification.** s02 read s01's commit with `git show --stat`, found it touched `app/greet.py` only, re-ran the t1 probe (exit 0) and judged that "the probe checks the criterion directly" (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain/20261004-005317/s02-handoff.md:10`). `chain next` re-ran the probes of every closed unit, all `pass` (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain/20261004-005317/s03-facts.md:9-12`). It also ran the gates on each change set: 0 blocking, 3 warnings, which were the unit files at 29 of the test bed's 30-line limit (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain/20261004-005317/s01-facts.md:8`).
- **Cost.** About 0.75 USD for the probe session and the four chain sessions, estimated from `sessions.jsonl` with the package's price rows (`_est_usd` in `package/.charter/bin/charter`).

What it changed before release:

- s02 handed over at 24%, under the 25% line, with nothing landed, and s03 paid the start-up again. `chain next` now refuses a `continue` below the line when the session committed nothing, unless `--early` says why (`cmd_next` in `package/.charter/chain.py`; `test_a_session_that_landed_nothing_works_on_up_to_the_hand_over_line` in `tests/test_chain_0_4_7.py`).
- On a 200k window, start-up alone takes about 16% (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain/probe-session.summary.json`), leaving 9 points of work before the line. `chain start` now warns under 500k (`test_a_small_window_is_named_at_the_start` in `tests/test_chain_0_4_7.py`).
- The first chain committed `app/__pycache__`, because the test bed had no `.gitignore` for it. The fault was the test bed's, not the chain's (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain/20261004-005204/s01-final-message.txt:12`). The line was added before the second chain.

## Cross-review, after the trials

A Codex review of the change (`codex exec review --uncommitted`) returned four findings, and the reviewing agent confirmed them and added three. All seven were fixed and pinned by tests in `tests/test_chain_0_4_7.py`:

- a launched session's join could be overwritten by the launcher's own write: `editing_link` in `package/.charter/chain.py` now locks;
- a `complete` handoff ended the chain even when the closed units' probes failed again, and now halts it (`test_halt_complete_stop_and_the_session_limit_end_the_chain`, `test_a_unit_closed_in_the_session_has_its_probes_rerun` in `tests/test_chain_0_4_7.py`);
- new untracked files were not counted as uncommitted work (`test_a_new_file_left_out_of_every_commit_is_uncommitted_work` in `tests/test_chain_0_4_7.py`);
- `chain next` did not check the branch (`test_a_session_on_another_branch_cannot_hand_over` in `tests/test_chain_0_4_7.py`);
- past the cap, `git ... > file`, `git ... | tee` and `$(...)` passed the allow-list (`test_past_the_cap_only_the_handoff_goes_through` in `tests/test_chain_0_4_7.py`);
- the hook's fast path ran outside its fail-open guard (`main` in `package/.charter/hooks/chain_gate.py`).
