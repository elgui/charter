# Trial record, 2026-10-04: a chain in cmux tabs whose sessions started past the hand-over line

Chain `20261004-011522` ran the 0.4.7 engine on the `bin/make-testbed` bed in the session scratchpad, with the spawn launcher in cmux, a 150,000-token window, hand-over at 25% and cap at 30%, `--model sonnet --effort low`, and the mission of the 0.4.7 trial (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/events.log:1`). It is the rerun that `docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain.md` § Visible tabs names: session 1 opened as a tab and stopped at the folder-trust menu. The owner answered the menu at 09:58, about 8 h 40 min after the start, and the chain then ran three sessions in two minutes (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/events.log:2-3`). The bed is not committed. The chain's event log, handoffs and verification reports are copied under `docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/`, with the screen of session 3's tab (cmux `surface:61`, read with `$ cmux read-screen --workspace workspace:9 --surface surface:61 --scrollback`) as `surface-61-screen.txt`.

## What happened

| session | start-up | what it did | record |
|---|---|---|---|
| s01 | 33% at its first tool | told to hand over 4 s after joining, capped 7 s later; committed one `## Log` line, handed over `continue` | `docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/events.log:3-6`; `docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/s01-handoff.md:26` |
| s02 | 33% | the same; its handoff says "The chain cannot progress at this budget", and still says `continue` | `docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/events.log:7-11`; `docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/s02-handoff.md:17` |
| s03 | 33% | refused at its first tool; three Bash commands refused at the cap, then a commit with a one-line message went through; handed over `halt` | `docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/surface-61-screen.txt:93-176` |

Start-up was 33% of 150,000, about 49,500 tokens, in all three sessions. The start had estimated 33,000 tokens (22%), the headless figure of the 0.4.7 trial (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/events.log:1`). An interactive tab loads more than `claude -p`, such as MCP server instructions. No line of the unit's code was written in three sessions.

## Defects, and what 0.4.8 does about them

| # | defect | fix | test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The engine relaunched sessions that started past the hand-over line. Each one could only hand over, and the next started the same way | the usage at a session's first tool is recorded as its start-up; a `continue` after a start-up at or past the line halts the chain with the figure (`started_past_line` in `package/.charter/chain.py`) | `test_a_session_that_starts_past_the_hand_over_line_halts_the_chain_and_resume_takes_a_new_budget` in `tests/test_chain_reliability.py` |
| 2 | Each session committed one `## Log` line. That counted as landing work, so the stall check (two sessions with no commit) never fired (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/s01-facts.md:2`, `docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/s02-facts.md:2`) | stalled means nothing changed outside `.taskets/` and no criterion checked (`stalled`, `nums.work`) | `test_sessions_that_commit_only_unit_files_are_stalled` |
| 3 | Past the cap, the hook refused the commit it tells the session to make. `git add .taskets/t1.md && git commit -qm "...` with the `Co-Authored-By: ... <address>` trailer was read as a redirection and a non-git line. Only `git commit -q -m "t1: log session 3 halt"` passed, so the trailer was lost (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/surface-61-screen.txt:153-168`) | the command is read with shell quoting; operators count only outside quotes, and the `"$(cat <<'EOF' ... EOF)"` message form passes. The refusal names what passes (`plain_commands`) | `test_a_commit_with_a_trailer_or_a_heredoc_message_goes_through_at_the_cap`, `test_plain_commands_reads_quotes_operators_and_newlines` |
| 4 | The halt told the owner to raise the budget and resume, but `charter chain resume` could not change the budget (`$ git show 38f0e56:package/.charter/chain.py | grep -n 'r.add_argument'`) | `chain resume --window`, `--wrap`, `--cap`, validated and recorded as a `budget` event | the test of row 1 |
| 5 | The start-up estimate in `chain start` was the headless one | the warning uses the largest start-up measured in the repository's latest chain, else an estimate by launcher (50,000 for spawn, 33,000 headless), and says when the hand-over line is under it (`measured_startup`, `STARTUP_TOKENS` in `package/.charter/chain.py`) | `test_the_start_names_a_hand_over_line_under_the_measured_start_up`, `test_the_start_estimates_the_start_up_by_launcher_when_none_was_measured` |

## Not defects

- The tab showed session 2's `## Next` and `## Watch out` twice. The briefing file holds each once (`.git/charter/chain/20261004-011522/s03/briefing.md` in the bed, `docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/s02-handoff.md:14-17`). The tab's screen redrew the pasted prompt, and nothing in the engine duplicated it.
- Session 1 waited at the folder-trust menu for 8 h 40 min. The chain reported `waiting`, as designed (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain.md` § Visible tabs).
- Each new session closed its predecessor's tab, and the last tab stayed open on the halt for the owner to read (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/events.log:12,18`).
- Sessions 2 and 3 opened as tabs from a session's own `chain next` once the folder was trusted, which tests the spawn launcher in cmux (`docs/trials/2026-10-04-chain-tab/events.log:7,14`). A tab opened by the detached review runner of 0.4.8 is still untested.
